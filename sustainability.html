01 · Energy

Renewable Energy Transition

Our flagship energy programme has transformed how we power our campuses and communities. By shifting to solar, wind, and biomass sources, we have cut our carbon footprint dramatically while inspiring neighbouring institutions to follow suit.

  • Solar Rooftops — 12,000 sq. ft. of panels installed across 4 campuses
  • Wind Partnerships — Tie-ups with 3 regional wind farms supplying clean kilowatts
  • Net Zero Target — On track to achieve full carbon neutrality by 2030
  • Community Microgrids — Powering 6 nearby villages with surplus green energy

02 · Forests

Campus Reforestation Drive

Trees are the lungs of our planet. Our reforestation programme engages students, faculty, and local communities in large-scale tree planting drives, restoring degraded land and creating vital green corridors for wildlife.

  • 250,000 Trees — Planted across Uttarakhand and adjoining states since 2020
  • Native Species — 80% of plantings use indigenous, climate-resilient varieties
  • Student Brigades — Over 8,000 student volunteers participate annually
  • Carbon Sequestration — Estimated 14,000 tonnes CO₂ absorbed per year

03 · Waste

Zero Waste Campus Programme

We are reimagining waste as a resource. Through segregation at source, composting, upcycling workshops, and partnerships with certified recyclers, we are systematically eliminating landfill dependency across all our facilities.

  • Segregation at Source — Colour-coded bins with smart sensors on all floors
  • Composting Units — 5 bio-composters turning 600 kg of organic waste daily
  • Plastic-Free Zones — Single-use plastic banned across all campus facilities
  • Upcycling Lab — Student-run workshop converting waste into usable products

04 · Water

Water Conservation & Harvesting

With water stress rising globally, our campus has become a model for responsible water stewardship. Rainwater harvesting, greywater recycling, and precision irrigation systems have slashed our freshwater consumption significantly.

  • Rainwater Harvesting — 18 collection points capturing 8M litres annually
  • Greywater Recycling — Treated and reused for irrigation and sanitation
  • Smart Irrigation — IoT-driven drip systems cut water use by 45%
  • Awareness Campaigns — Monthly workshops reaching 2,500+ students
