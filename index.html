About the Awards

Honoring Legacy & Impact

A tribute to the persistent efforts and remarkable contributions of our alumni in every corner of the world.

🏛️

The Vision

These awards are a tribute to the persistent efforts and remarkable contributions of our alumni in their respective fields — celebrating those who carry the spirit of Graphic Era to every corner of the globe.

🌟

The Event

Winners will be honored during the prestigious Alumni Awards Ceremony — a landmark gathering of Graphic Era's most accomplished alumni, leaders, and visionaries.

📍 Alumni Awards Ceremony - Dehradun'2027

Nomination Guidelines

How to Nominate

Read all guidelines carefully before submitting. Nominations are assessed by the GEAA judging committee based on evidence and criteria.

👤

Who Can Nominate

  • Alumni may submit a Self-Nomination.
  • Alumni may nominate a Fellow Alumnus/a.
  • Staff & faculty members may also nominate alumni.
  • A nominator may propose multiple alumni across different categories.
📋

Submission Requirements

  • Review all award categories before nominating.
  • Provide relevant proof(s) supporting the nomination.
  • Clearly describe the nominee's qualities, contributions, and worthiness.
  • All mandatory fields in the Nomination Form must be completed.
⚖️

Judging Process

  • Nominations are judged on award criteria and evidence provided.
  • The GEAA judging committee reviews all eligible applications.
  • Winners will be honoured at the Alumni Awards Ceremony - 2027.
📅

Key Deadline

Feb 15, 2027

All nominations must be submitted before this date. Late submissions will not be considered.

Nominate Now ◆
Judging Criteria

Four Pillars of Evaluation

01

Impact

Evidence of tangible, measurable impact — and its scale — in your profession, community, or society, inspired by your GEU education.

02

GU Influence

Evidence of how your Graphic Era education played a key role in shaping your success and amplifying your impact.

03

Media Traction

The degree to which your success story is likely to resonate with local, national, or global media in your country of entry or beyond.

04

Inspire Potential

Your capacity and willingness to influence the next generation of students to choose Graphic Era as their study destination.

About Us
What is the Alumni Association?
Our Alumni Association is a thriving community dedicated to fostering lifelong connections between graduates and the institution. We empower alumni through mentorship, networking, scholarships, and cultural engagement — across campuses and across the globe.

Lifelong Membership

Automatic membership upon graduation, with access to networks, events, and resources for life.

Mentorship

Connect senior alumni with current students and fresh graduates to guide careers and growth.

Scholarships & Grants

Alumni-funded scholarships supporting meritorious students from all walks of life.

Global Events

Regular meetups, webinars, and forums spanning every continent our alumni call home.

Career & Placement

Referrals, job boards, and exclusive hiring events powered by the alumni network.
